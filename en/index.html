Children & sleeping arrangements

Children of all ages are welcome. A free cot for ages 0–2 can be requested, subject to the selected unit and availability. No extra beds are listed. Guests aged 18 and over count as adults.

House guidelines

Pets and parties/events are not allowed. There is no swimming pool.
